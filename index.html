Choose No to disable automatic emails for the rally, including expense reminders. Members can still enter their expenses in the app.

⏰ Automatic Email Schedule
  • Rally Response Check & Update: sent on the 1st of every month to all active memberships.
  • No response: the selected number of days before cut-off (1–60; default 7).
  • MAYBE follow-up: 3 days before cut-off.
  • Booking ticket & Park report: produced together on the selected number of days after cut-off (0–30). The Park Report is sent only when Automatic Park Report is Yes and a Park Email is entered.
  • Deposit: every 7 days after the booking ticket, until the member confirms payment or the rally begins.
  • Expenses: 2 days after the rally finishes, for recorded attendees who have not submitted expenses.
  • Coordinator report: immediately after each email batch.
Important: Automatic Reminders must be Yes when the daily check runs. Expense reminders can be enabled later, but only within 30 days after the rally finishes and after the actual attendees have been recorded.
